The settlement math

The application uses the same basic idea behind debt simplification tools: reduce unnecessary payment paths without changing anyone's total net balance. Splitwise documents this as “Simplify Debts.”

💳

Paid

Total amount a member actually paid for group expenses.

⚖️

Owed

Total amount assigned to that member by the expense splits.

🧮

Net balance

Paid minus owed. Positive means receive; negative means pay.

Example

If A is owed ₹2,000 and B owes ₹2,000, SplitSmart can represent that as one direct payment from B → A instead of carrying multiple intermediate debts.

Split methods

Modern expense-sharing products support equal and uneven splits; Splitwise also documents exact amount, percentage and shares-based splits.
