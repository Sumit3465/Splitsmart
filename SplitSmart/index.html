Features

Everything a group needs to stay square

From tracking the first coffee run to closing out the whole trip, SplitSmart keeps every rupee accounted for.

🧾

Smart Expense Tracking

Log expenses by category, date, and payer in seconds, with a full searchable history for every group.

⚖️

Equal & Custom Splits

Split evenly, enter exact amounts, or divide by percentage — SplitSmart validates every split for you.

🧮

Settlement Optimisation

An automatic algorithm collapses tangled debts into a simplified set of payments between members.

🔒

Local Data Storage

Everything lives in your browser's LocalStorage — no accounts, no servers, no data leaving your device.

📊

Expense Analytics

Visualise spending by category, see who's contributing the most, and track monthly totals at a glance.

👥

Flexible Groups

Create as many groups as you need — trips, roommates, events — each with its own members and ledger.

Built for Phase 1

More than an expense form

SplitSmart combines forms, validation, arrays, objects, DOM updates, LocalStorage and a settlement algorithm into one practical JavaScript application.

🧠

Transparent calculations

See how paid, owed and net balances turn into settlement instructions.

💾

Local-first

No backend is required for Phase 1. Your data stays in this browser.

📱

Responsive experience

Groups, expenses, settlement and help content are designed for desktop and mobile.
