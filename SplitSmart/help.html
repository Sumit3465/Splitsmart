HELP CENTER

Answers before the money gets confusing.

Phase 1 is intentionally local and educational. These answers explain what the current build does, what it does not do, and how to protect your data.

Where is my data stored?

Groups, expenses, settlements, profile information and theme preferences are stored in your browser using LocalStorage. There is no backend database in Phase 1.

Why do I have Sign Up and Sign In if there is no backend?

They are local profile/onboarding pages for the Phase 1 experience. They do not provide production authentication and do not send credentials to a server.

Can I use the app on another laptop?

Not automatically. LocalStorage belongs to the current browser/device. Use the backup tools in Profile & Settings to move your ledger manually.

What happens when I load Demo Data?

SplitSmart creates a sample Goa Trip 2026 group with sample members and expenses. Demo people are sample data, not your local account.

How does settlement optimisation work?

The app calculates each member's net balance, separates people who need to pay from people who need to receive, and matches those balances into a simplified set of transfers.

Can I split an expense unevenly?

Yes. Phase 1 supports equal, custom amount and percentage splits, with validation and cent-level reconciliation.

Can I pay someone from SplitSmart?

No. Phase 1 records settlement instructions and settlement status locally. Payment gateways and real-time payment verification are outside this syllabus phase.

What should I do before clearing browser data?

Export a backup first. Clearing site data can permanently remove your LocalStorage ledger.
