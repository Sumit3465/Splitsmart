USE CASES

One ledger. Many kinds of groups.

Expense-sharing apps commonly target trips, roommates, couples, restaurant bills and other recurring group situations. Tricount explicitly highlights these use cases, while Splitwise describes groups for apartments, trips and family vacations.

✈️

Group trips

Hotels, taxis, meals, tickets and shopping can all live in one trip ledger.

🏠

Roommates

Track groceries, utilities, rent-related shared costs and household purchases.

🍕

Restaurant bills

Handle equal or uneven shares when everyone ordered something different.

🎉

Events

Manage birthdays, festivals, parties and shared event purchases.

🎓

Student groups

Track project materials, trips, society events and team purchases.

❤️

Couples & families

Use transparent splits for shared meals, travel and household costs.
